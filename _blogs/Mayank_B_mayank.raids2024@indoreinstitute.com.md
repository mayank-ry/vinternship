Currently going through the ViBE MERN Stack Vinternship at IIT Ropar with VLED Labs, and it’s been a very different experience.

It’s not just about learning MERN — it’s more about building consistency and discipline.

One of the best parts of this internship has been the Regular Standups. We connect on Zoom, discuss our progress, share thoughts, and speak up. It really helped me improve my confidence, communication, and understanding at the same time.

During this internship, I also got a glimpse of the IIT culture — how people learn, think, and approach problem-solving.Being part of the Discord community where mentors encourage helping each other made learning more collaborative and actually enjoyable.

The case studies and discussions pushed me to think more like a developer instead of just completing tasks. And that shift in mindset is what makes this journey meaningful.

Still learning, still improving… and looking forward to what’s next 

& Special Thanks To All The Mentors.
